Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-23

This policy explains what data WattFigures (wattfigures.com) collects, why, and the choices you have. Responsible party: WattFigures — secforgeweb@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Server logs: our hosting provider records IP address, user agent and requested URLs for security and error diagnosis. These logs are held by our static-hosting provider for a short period (typically a few weeks) and are not used to build profiles of visitors.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics (GA4) to see which pages are useful, in aggregate — never to build an individual profile of you. It only runs after you accept it in the cookie banner; if you decline, no analytics data is collected. See the cookie policy for exactly what that involves.
  • Contact: if you email us, we keep the correspondence only to answer you.
  • Newsletter: we do not run a newsletter and do not collect email addresses.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Once ads serve, this site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com. A list of third-party vendors is available at Google's ad technology providers page.

Consent

Every visitor is shown a cookie-consent banner (built by us, using Google's Consent Mode) before analytics runs, and — once ads go live — before any advertising cookies are set either. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the "Cookies" link in the site footer, or on the cookie policy page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. To exercise any right, email secforgeweb@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change and adjust the "last updated" date above.
