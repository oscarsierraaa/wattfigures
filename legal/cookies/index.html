Managing consent

Once analytics are switched on, every visitor sees a consent banner on first visit — built by us, using Google's Consent Mode, not a third-party pop-up — offering to accept or decline analytics. Nothing beyond the essential cookies above is set until you accept. You can change your mind any time from the "Cookies" link in the site footer, which reopens the same banner. When advertising goes live, the same mechanism will extend to ad-personalisation consent. You can also clear or block cookies from your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.
