Calculators

Instant answers: type your numbers — wattage, hours, price per kWh — and see the running cost for any appliance in your home, right away.

Some calculators below compare two options directly — a heat pump against a gas boiler, LED against halogen, solar panels against your own bill — rather than pricing a single appliance on its own.

Solar Panel Payback Calculator

Estimate how many years a home solar system takes to pay for itself from its size, cost, yearly yield, self-consumption and your electricity and export prices.

Washer and Dryer Running Cost Calculator

Estimate what your washing machine and tumble dryer cost per cycle, per week and per year from their energy per cycle and your electricity price.

LED vs Halogen Savings Calculator

See how much you save each year by switching bulbs to LED, and how fast the change pays for itself, based on wattages, hours and your electricity price.

Heat Pump vs Gas Boiler Cost Calculator

Compare a year of heating with a heat pump versus a gas boiler using your heat demand, the heat pump COP, boiler efficiency and fuel prices.

Air Conditioning Running Cost Calculator

See what your air conditioner costs to run per hour, per day and per cooling season from its power draw, daily hours and your electricity price.

EV Home Charging Cost Calculator

Estimate what charging your electric car at home costs — per full charge, per 100 km and per year — from your battery, efficiency and price per kWh.

Standby Power Calculator: The Yearly Cost of Vampire Devices

Add up what your always-on gadgets cost while 'off'. Estimate the yearly and monthly cost of standby (vampire) power across your home.

Electricity Cost Calculator: What Any Appliance Costs to Run

Work out what any appliance costs to run per day, month and year from its wattage, hours of use and your price per kWh.
